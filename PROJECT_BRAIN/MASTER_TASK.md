# WOB ART Modernization

Transform WOB ART into a premium automotive wrapping experience with cinematic art direction, clear conversion UX, strong portfolio storytelling, accessible motion, and production-grade frontend engineering. Preserve real business data and existing functionality while removing UX anti-patterns and consolidating the design language.

## Principles
- One dominant accent plus calibrated neutrals.
- Editorial automotive composition over generic SaaS cards.
- Motion must reinforce hierarchy; no scroll-jacking.
- Use existing imagery first; optimize it through Next Image.
- Mobile is a first-class composition.
- Never invent business claims or pricing.
