# WOB ART Visual System

## Palette
- Obsidian `#0A0A0A`
- Carbon `#111111`
- Acid accent `#E8FF00`
- Primary text `#F0F0F0`
- Muted text `#8A8A8A`
- Hairline `rgba(255,255,255,0.10)`

## Type
- Display: Bebas Neue
- Label: Barlow Condensed
- Body: DM Sans
- Mono: JetBrains Mono

## Composition
- 4/8px spacing rhythm
- thin rules and generous negative space
- mostly square geometry; radius reserved for functional controls
- editorial asymmetric grids
- motion primarily through opacity/transform/clip-path

## Motion
- fast micro-interaction ~160–220ms
- reveal ~600–800ms
- hero / portfolio transitions may be slower
- reduced motion disables non-essential transforms and marquee
